OPEN OBSERVATORY CONTROL

One link between your astronomy hardware and your software.

OpenAstroLink is an open, node-first control layer for cameras, mounts, focusers, plate solving, autofocus, guiding and automated observing workflows.

View on GitHubHow it works

Native hardware

QHY, Canon EOS, EQDrive/Sky-Watcher, Gemini and ZWO ASI/EAF plus compatibility backends behind one control model.

Live acquisition

Live View, FITS/RAW science capture, SER recording with metadata, histogram-assisted exposure and scene/star autofocus.

Astrometry & automation

Plate solving, adaptive urban capture, polar alignment and resource-locked asynchronous operations execute on the node.

v0.2.10.48

One plan can now execute DSO and planetary capture.

The node accepts ObservationPlan / ObservationBlock schedules. DSO blocks perform slew → adaptive plate solve/recenter → autofocus → FITS/RAW capture. Planetary blocks perform GOTO → full-frame planet acquisition → optional planet autofocus → hardware ROI → finite raw SER, with ROI tracking and frame-indexed sensor-region provenance. Optional calibrated mount recentering is implemented but remains HIL-sensitive and off by default.

ObservationPlan
  └─ DSO block
      ├─ Slew
      ├─ Solve / recenter until tolerance
      ├─ Autofocus when due
      └─ FITS / RAW × N

NODE FIRST

Keep control close to the hardware.

The OpenAstroLink node owns device sessions and long-running operations. GUIs and integrations communicate over HTTP/WebSocket, while Stellarium uses its telescope-control bridge for live mount position and GOTO.
