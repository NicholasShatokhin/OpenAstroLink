OPEN · NATIVE-FIRST · NODE-FIRST

One control layer for an astronomy setup.

OpenAstroLink connects cameras, mounts and focusers to a node-owned workflow engine for acquisition, plate solving, autofocus, guiding foundations and scheduled observing. Native OAL drivers are the default; INDI is optional compatibility.

v0.2.10.50 removes the temporary hidden EQDrive maxNativeGotoDeg / 15° qualification gate. Normal RA/DEC GOTO is no longer artificially capped inside the driver. Operator-controlled Core/profile sky-safety remains available, and raw-axis calls retain an explicit mechanical guard.

OFFLINE NAVIGATION · v0.2.10.51

A simple sky map is built into OpenAstroSuite.

The left workspace now includes an offline horizon map for telescope navigation without Stellarium. Search bright stars and selected Messier/DSO targets, pan and zoom, see the live telescope pointing, plate-solved center and approximate camera FOV, then Slew, Sync, Abort, Park/Unpark or send the target into Scheduler.

All mount actions still go through the active OpenAstroLink backend and the existing coordinate/safety path. The HIL-qualified direct-MC v9 geometry is not duplicated or changed.

NEAREST BETA

Now qualify workflows, not more build plumbing.

  1. HIL autofocusConvergence, repeatability, backlash, cancel/failure rollback and a final verification frame.
  2. Auto-exposure HILConvergence, lock and reacquisition after a real illumination change.
  3. Scheduler HILMixed blocks, cancellation and restart-at-block-boundary behavior.
  4. Mosaic HILTile geometry, solve/recenter and traversal.
  5. Polar Alignment HILReal-sky sampling and safe-region motion.

Smart Telescope UX is deliberately an OAL 1.0 track, not a requirement for the nearest Beta.

NATIVE FIRST

Keep hardware ownership close to the hardware.

openastrolink-node owns device sessions and long-running operations. Local or remote GUIs use HTTP/WebSocket; Stellarium uses the telescope bridge. Native OAL drivers are enabled by default. INDI is opt-in for compatibility or hardware that does not yet have a native driver.

Road to OAL 1.0

Unattended-observatory hardening remains separate from the supervised Beta: durable mid-block checkpoints, production guiding, meridian-flip recovery, weather/roof/power safety, TLS/auth/roles/audit, reliable event replay, driver isolation, durable science provenance and Smart Telescope UX.
